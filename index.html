Welcome

British Columbia is a paradise for travelers, offering a mix of wild landscapes and vibrant cities. Start in Vancouver, where you can stroll the Stanley Park seawall, explore Granville Island, and ski or hike the North Shore mountains all in one day. Take the ferry to Victoria to wander the Inner Harbour and the Butchart Gardens, or head up the Sea-to-Sky Highway to Whistler for world-class skiing in winter and mountain biking in summer. For a laid-back getaway, the Okanagan Valley's sunny beaches and award-winning wineries are hard to beat, while Tofino on Vancouver Island rewards visitors with surf, rainforest trails, and storm-watching. Wherever you go, BC delivers the kind of scenery that makes you want to stay a little longer.
